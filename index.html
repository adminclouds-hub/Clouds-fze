About us

A trading partner you can rely on.

Clouds FZE LLC is a UAE-based wholesale and general trading company. We connect buyers with reliable manufacturers and brand owners for packaged foodstuff and beverages, handling sourcing, pricing, documentation and delivery.

Our focus markets are the United Arab Emirates and Bangladesh, and we source internationally to match each buyer's range, budget and compliance needs.
